Background

Hawker centres are open-air food courts with dozens of independent stalls. At lunch, office workers queue at several stalls and wait at their tables for their queue numbers. HawkerHub lets diners order and pay from their phones for any stalls in a centre, sends tickets to each stall's tablet and shows queue numbers on screens in the centre.

Stall owners are small businesses with thin margins: they pay a small commission per order and expect next-day settlement that matches their sales to the cent. The platform's value grows with the number of stalls and diners on it.

You are the Lead Solution Architect. Two pilot centres ran on a prototype; Phase 1 rolls out to all 120 centres. Phase 2 adds delivery through partner riders and recommendations.

1.Phase 1 of the Platform

Actors:

  1. Diner: orders and pays through the HawkerHub PWA or app.
  2. Stall Owner: receives tickets on a stall tablet, marks orders ready and manages the menu.
  3. Centre Manager: oversees a centre's stalls and screens.
  4. Finance Officer: reviews settlements and reconciliations.
  5. Queue Display: a screen at the centre showing ready queue numbers.
  6. Payment Provider: an external provider for national QR payments and cards.
Use CaseDescription
Browse MenusThe Diner browses stalls and menus in a centre, including sold-out status.
Place OrderThe Diner places one order with items from one or more stalls and pays; each stall gets a ticket; the Diner gets one queue number per stall.
Receive TicketThe stall tablet shows new tickets.
Mark Ready / CollectedThe Stall Owner marks a ticket ready; the queue number appears on the centre's displays; later collected.
Mark Sold OutThe Stall Owner marks an item sold out or available again.
RefundThe Stall Owner refunds an item that cannot be served.
Settle StallsEach night the platform computes each stall's takings minus commission and refunds, and pays out by 06:00 (T+1).

2.Phase 2 of the Platform

Use CaseDescription
Order for DeliveryDiners order for delivery; two external delivery partners accept and dispatch riders via their APIs.
RecommendDiners see recommendations based on past orders and time of day.

3.Extensibility of the Platform

New delivery partners, payment methods and centre types (e.g. food courts in malls) must be addable without changing the order flow.

4.Performance, Scalability and Availability

  • 120 centres, 6,000 stalls, 1.2 million registered diners.
  • 400,000 orders per day; 45% of them between 11:45 and 13:15; between 12:00 and 12:10 the order rate is double the lunch-window average.
  • Place Order, including payment authorisation: under 3 s for 95% of orders.
  • A new ticket must appear on the stall tablet within 5 s of payment for 99% of tickets; queue displays update within 3 s.
  • Menus are browsed about 10× as often as orders are placed; sold-out changes must be visible to diners within 30 s.
  • Stall tablets are on centre Wi-Fi, which drops for up to 10 minutes at a time.
  • 99.9% availability during meal peaks (11:00–14:00, 17:30–20:30).

5.Security

  • Payments use provider tokens; HawkerHub never stores card numbers.
  • Diners' phone numbers and names are not shown to stalls; stalls see only a queue number and the items.
  • Stall tablets are bound to a stall and unlocked with the owner's PIN.
