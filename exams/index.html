Exams

SWE5001 Architecting Scalable Systems: the written exam for all three courses (Architecting, Platform Engineering, Cloud Native Solution Design).

The exam is one paper with three sections and 50 marks, built around a case study handed out as Appendix A. Every question asks for a judgement about that case: defend a choice, critique a model, diagnose a symptom, or fix a design and name what the fix costs. No question asks for a definition.

Suggested order: read What the exam tests first. Then do the sample paper against its case study, with the case open in a second tab. Then sit the mock papers timed, one per sitting, before opening any model answer.
