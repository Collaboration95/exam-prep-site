Background

Motor insurers compete on how quickly and fairly they settle claims. ClaimSure is a mid-sized motor insurer with 1.2 million policies whose claims process is still largely manual: policyholders phone in, surveyors visit workshops, and adjusters re-key data into the policy system on the mainframe.

ClaimSure wants a claims platform connecting policyholders, partner workshops, surveyors and adjusters, with AI that estimates repair costs from photos and an assistant that answers policyholders' questions from their policy wording. Storms and floods produce sudden surges of claims that the current process cannot absorb.

You are the Lead Solution Architect. Phase 1 digitises the claim lifecycle with AI-assisted estimates; Phase 2 adds the GenAI assistant, straight-through processing for small claims and fraud-network analytics. The policy administration system stays on the mainframe.

1.Phase 1 of the Platform

Actors:

  1. Policyholder: reports an accident in the app, uploads photos and tracks the claim.
  2. Workshop: a partner repair garage that receives assigned claims and submits repair quotes.
  3. Adjuster: reviews claims, AI estimates and quotes, and approves or rejects.
  4. Surveyor: inspects vehicles on site when needed.
  5. Fraud Analyst: investigates flagged claims.
  6. Policy System: the on-premises mainframe that holds policies and cover (POLICY_MASTER).
  7. AI Provider: an external vision and language model service in an approved region. Bank: pays workshops and policyholders.
Use CaseDescription
Report AccidentThe Policyholder records time, place and description and uploads 10–30 photos; the platform acknowledges with a claim number.
Check CoverThe platform checks the policy is valid and covers the event.
Assess DamageThe platform produces an AI repair estimate from the photos; the Adjuster reviews it.
Assign WorkshopThe Adjuster or Policyholder chooses a partner workshop.
Submit / Withdraw QuoteThe Workshop submits a repair quote, or withdraws it.
Approve ClaimThe Adjuster approves the claim and amount, or rejects it, recording a reason.
Screen for FraudRules flag suspicious claims to the Fraud Analyst.
PayThe platform pays the workshop or the policyholder through the bank.
Track ClaimThe Policyholder sees status and messages.

2.Phase 2 of the Platform

Use CaseDescription
Ask AssistantA GenAI assistant answers policyholders' questions grounded in their policy wording and claim status.
Straight-through ProcessingClaims under $2,000 that pass all checks can be approved without an adjuster.
Analyse Fraud NetworksLinks between parties, vehicles and workshops across claims are analysed to detect organised fraud.

3.Extensibility of the Platform

New AI models and prompts, new partner types (towing, car rental) and new claim types (windscreen) must be addable without changing the core lifecycle.

4.Performance, Scalability and Capacity

  • Normally about 600 claims/day; after a storm up to 8,000 claims in 24 hours with a peak hour of 1,400.
  • 10–30 photos per claim (average 20) at about 3 MB each.
  • Report Accident acknowledged in under 5 s for 95% of reports, including during surges.
  • AI estimate available within 2 minutes for 90% of claims.
  • 250 concurrent adjusters.
  • The mainframe policy system accepts at most 20 transactions per second and is available 06:00–23:00 only.
  • The AI provider allows 300 requests per minute; a single-image request costs about $0.04; the AI budget is $3,000 per day.

5.Security and Compliance

  • Claim decisions must be explainable and auditable for 7 years, including any AI input used.
  • Personal data sent to AI providers must be minimised and stay in the approved region; provider keys never leave ClaimSure's internal zone.
  • Workshops may only access claims assigned to them and only the data needed to repair the vehicle.
