Background

Online retail in the region has grown every year, and so has the cost of the last mile: failed doorstep deliveries, repeat attempts and couriers circling for parking. Loop Logistics is a start-up that wants to cut those costs with a delivery platform called ParcelPath. It combines courier routing with a network of smart parcel lockers at train stations, malls and housing estates.

ParcelPath is a platform rather than a single application: merchants create shipments through an API, independent couriers accept delivery jobs, recipients track and collect parcels, and locker sites are operated by hub operators. The more merchants join, the more parcels flow to the lockers, which makes the network more attractive to couriers and recipients.

You are the Lead Solution Architect. Phase 1 (the MVP) must be live before the year-end sale season. Phase 2 adds dynamic re-routing and ETA prediction.

There are 4 pilot applications: a mobile app for couriers, a web portal for merchants, a web tracking page for recipients and a web console for hub operators and platform admins. Locker controllers run embedded software supplied with the lockers but configurable by the platform.

1.Phase 1 of the Platform

Actors:

  1. Merchant: an online shop that creates shipments through the Merchant API or portal and pays monthly.
  2. Recipient: receives a parcel at home or at a locker; tracks it on the web page; does not need an account.
  3. Courier: an independent driver who accepts daily routes in the courier app, scans parcels and deposits them in lockers or at doorsteps.
  4. Hub Operator: manages locker stations, clears expired parcels and handles returns.
  5. Platform Admin: onboards merchants and couriers.
  6. Locker Controller: the embedded controller in each locker station (keypad, compartments, 4G router).
  7. SMS Gateway, Payment Gateway, Maps Service: external systems for SMS, payouts and invoices, and road distances.
Use CaseDescription
Create ShipmentThe Merchant submits recipient details and one or more parcels; the platform returns a shipment number and label.
Plan RoutesEvery day at 05:00 the platform groups the day's parcels into routes per zone and courier, using road distances from the Maps Service.
Accept RouteThe Courier accepts the route offered for the day.
Scan ParcelThe Courier scans each parcel at pickup and at each stop.
Deposit Parcel in LockerAt a locker station the Courier selects the parcel; the platform allocates a free compartment of a suitable size, the compartment opens, the Courier deposits the parcel and closes it. A pickup code is generated and sent to the Recipient by SMS.
Collect ParcelThe Recipient enters the pickup code at the locker keypad; the compartment opens. The code is single-use and expires after 72 hours.
Track ShipmentThe Recipient or Merchant views status, ETA and, while the parcel is out for delivery, the courier's live position.
Report Failed DeliveryThe Courier records a failed doorstep delivery; the parcel is redirected to the nearest locker.
Clear Expired ParcelsThe Hub Operator removes expired parcels from lockers and returns them to the hub.
Pay Couriers / Invoice MerchantsWeekly courier payouts and monthly merchant invoices are produced.

2.Phase 2 of the Platform

Use CaseDescription
Re-route on IncidentWhen a traffic incident affects a route, the platform recomputes the affected couriers' remaining stops and pushes revised routes within 60 seconds.
Predict ETAThe platform predicts each parcel's delivery window from historical routes, traffic and courier behaviour.
Reserve LockerRecipients may reserve a locker compartment at a preferred station before dispatch.

3.Extensibility of the Platform

The platform must allow new locker vendors, new courier types (e.g. cargo bikes) and further merchant integrations to be added without changing existing services.

4.Performance, Scalability and Availability

  • 2,000 merchants; 150,000 parcels per day on normal days and 300,000 on sale days, with 30% created between 10:00 and 12:00.
  • Up to 5,000 couriers active concurrently; each courier app sends one location ping every 10 seconds while on a route.
  • 800 locker stations with up to 60 compartments each; up to 15 couriers may be depositing at one station at the same time.
  • A compartment must open within 2 seconds for 95% of deposit and collection attempts.
  • The tracking page must load in under 2 seconds for 90% of requests and show a courier position no older than 30 seconds.
  • Locker collection must continue to work during a network outage of up to 4 hours.
  • Locker operations (deposit, collect) must achieve 99.95% monthly availability; other features 99.9%.

5.Security

  • Pickup codes are single-use secrets and must not be recoverable from a stolen locker controller.
  • Recipients' personal data (name, phone, address) and couriers' location histories are personal data under the national data protection act; only the minimum needed may be shared with merchants and couriers.
  • Merchant API access is authenticated per merchant and rate-limited.
