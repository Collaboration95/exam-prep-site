Background

Electric vehicles are a fast-growing share of new car sales, and drivers need public chargers they can find, start and pay for easily. ChargeGrid operates a network of charging stations at malls, offices and car parks owned by site hosts, who set their own tariffs and earn a share of revenue.

ChargeGrid wants to become a platform: its own drivers should be able to charge on partner networks, partner networks' drivers on ChargeGrid stations (roaming), and sites should share limited grid capacity intelligently (smart charging). The local utility will pay sites to cut load during grid stress (demand response).

You are the Lead Solution Architect. Phase 1 replaces the prototype; Phase 2 adds roaming and smart charging.

1.Phase 1 of the Platform

Actors:

  1. Driver: registers in the app, adds a vehicle and a payment method, finds and starts chargers.
  2. Fleet Manager: manages a company's vehicles and receives a monthly invoice.
  3. Site Host: owns a site; sets tariffs and views revenue.
  4. Operations Engineer: monitors stations and resets them remotely.
  5. Charging Station: a field device with one to four connectors, connected over 4G or site Ethernet using a standard station protocol over WebSocket.
  6. Payment Gateway: an external card payment service (tokenised cards). Utility: the grid operator (Phase 2).
Use CaseDescription
Find ChargerThe Driver searches for available connectors near a location.
Reserve ConnectorThe Driver holds a connector for up to 15 minutes.
Start Charging SessionThe Driver scans the station's QR code; the platform authorises the driver and payment method, fixes the price and commands the station to start. The station confirms within a few seconds (up to 20 s on weak connections).
Report Meter ValuesDuring a session the station reports energy delivered every 30 seconds.
Stop Session and BillThe Driver or the vehicle stops the session; the platform computes the cost (energy plus idle fee), issues a receipt and captures payment.
Manage TariffsThe Site Host sets the price per kWh and idle fees for its site; changes take effect immediately for new sessions.
Monitor and Reset StationOperations views station health and sends remote resets.
Invoice FleetMonthly invoices for fleet customers.

2.Phase 2 of the Platform

Use CaseDescription
RoamUp to 12 partner networks start and stop sessions for their drivers through a Partner API, and ChargeGrid drivers charge on partners' stations.
Smart ChargingThe platform keeps each site under its grid capacity by sending charging limits (profiles) to connectors.
Demand ResponseWhen the utility signals grid stress, participating sites must reduce load within 60 seconds.

3.Extensibility of the Platform

New station vendors, new partner networks and new tariff models (time-of-day pricing) must be addable without changing the core session flow.

4.Performance, Scalability and Availability

  • 3,500 stations with 9,000 connectors; heartbeats every 60 s; meter values every 30 s per active connector.
  • 25,000 sessions/day; up to 1,800 concurrent sessions at the evening peak (18:00–21:00).
  • Start Charging Session: from tap to charging confirmed in under 5 s for 95% of attempts.
  • Roaming: up to 12 partners, each up to 50 requests/s.
  • Start and stop must be 99.9% available; demand-response reduction within 60 s.
  • 200,000 registered drivers.

5.Security

  • Card data must never be stored or processed by ChargeGrid systems; only gateway tokens.
  • Each station authenticates with its own certificate; stations in the field are considered physically accessible to attackers.
  • Partners authenticate per partner; partner calls are audited.
